A computer-vision deployment project: fine-tuned **YOLOv11** and ran it on an **edge device**
to check personal protective equipment compliance on a construction site — detecting whether
workers are wearing **helmets, safety vests and gloves**.

On top of the detector we built an **admin dashboard** that tracks worker density across the
site and sends **email notifications** when a hazard or a compliance violation is detected.

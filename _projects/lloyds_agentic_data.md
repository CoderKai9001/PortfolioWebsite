**3rd place at the Lloyds Banking Group internal hackathon** (Dec 2025), which opened a few
slots to teams from IIIT Hyderabad.

We built an **agentic AI system** on Google Cloud that detects inconsistencies across a data
estate and proposes corrections. The design point was the **human in the loop**: in a bank,
an agent that silently rewrites records is a liability, so every proposed repair is surfaced
for a person to approve, reject or amend before anything is committed.

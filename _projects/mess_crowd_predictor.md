**Most Impactful Project award at HackIIIT 2026.** We did not place in the top three, but this
is the project of mine that most people actually use.

IIIT Hyderabad has three messes, and students entering scan a QR code tagged to them. That
scan stream is a live crowd sensor nobody was reading. The app:

- counts scans in the **last 10 minutes** to show how long the QR queue is right now,
- counts **total scans** to show how full the mess itself is, and
- fits an **ARMA** model over historical scan data to recommend a good time to go next.

It has since been **integrated into the official MyIIIT app** and is used daily by **1000+
students** — especially when a special meal has everyone arriving at once.

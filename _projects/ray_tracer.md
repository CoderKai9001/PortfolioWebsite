A **ray tracer** built from first principles in **C++**, rendered to an OpenGL display.

It implements analytic ray–primitive intersection for five primitives — **spheres, cylinders,
cuboids, ellipsoids and cones** — and the full pipeline on top: camera ray generation,
lighting, shading and shadows.

Research

01

Topological mapping for indoor navigation

Ongoing

Robots that navigate real indoor spaces need a map that captures structure, not just geometry. I work on topological representations paired with end-to-end learned policies, so a robot can plan over places and doorways rather than over a metric grid it can never keep perfectly accurate.
